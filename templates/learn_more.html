How EcoTrack calculates your footprint

  • Transport: distance (km/month) × mode emission factor × 12
  • Electricity: kWh/month × 0.82 × 12
  • LPG: cylinders/month × 42 × 12
  • Diet: fixed annual baseline by diet type, plus meat-meal add-on
  • Waste: bag factor × bags/week × 52
  • Offsets: trees × 21/yr, plus fixed offsets per sustainable habit
